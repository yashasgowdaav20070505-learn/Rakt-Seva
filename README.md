## 📖 App Description

**Rakt Seva** is a state-of-the-art, dual-mode emergency blood donation platform designed to bridge the critical time gap between blood requesters and eligible donors. Operating as a hyper-local life-saving ecosystem, Rakt Seva provides real-time geographic mapping, verified donor networks, blood bank inventory tracking, and organized donation drive management.

Whether you are an individual looking to donate blood at a nearby camp or a hospital/family in urgent need of rare blood groups during a emergency, Rakt Seva dynamically adapts its interface to suit your immediate context—ensuring zero confusion and lightning-fast action when every second counts.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Highlights

### 🔄 1. Context-Aware Dual Modes (Donor Mode vs. Requester Mode)
* **Donor Mode**:
  * **Focused Quick Actions**: Streamlined specifically for donors—includes **Donation Camps**, **Blood Banks Directory**, and quick mode toggle to request blood.
  * **Donation Camps Hub**: Browse scheduled mega drives organized by NGOs, Rotary Clubs, and College NSS units with full venue address, timing, partner hospital blood bank, and RSVP options.
  * **Digital Donor Profile & cooling-Off Tracker**: Keeps track of last donation date, eligible next donation date, blood group certification, and earned badges.
* **Requester Mode**:
  * **Nearby Donor Directory (SOS Radar)**: Replaces emergency distraction with an actionable list of nearby verified donors matching the requested blood group.
  * **Instant Request Dispatch**: Send direct SMS/Call blood alerts to nearby available donors with one tap.
  * **Hospital & Blood Bank Availability**: Live stock visibility for Whole Blood, PRBC, Platelets, FFP, and Cryoprecipitate.

---

### 🗺️ 2. Google Maps Interactive Radar
* **Realistic Google Maps Interface**: Powered by the official Google Maps API with support for Roadmap, Satellite, Terrain view, Search Bar, and Current Location pinpointing.
* **Mode-Driven Dynamic Map Pinning**:
  * In **Donor Mode**: Pinpoints nearby **Donation Camps** and critical **Emergency Donation Sites**.
  * In **Requester Mode**: Highlights active **Nearby Donors** and **Licensed Blood Banks**.
* **Interactive Marker Popups**: Click any pin to open modal sheets containing donor credentials, verification status, distance, contact details, and route directions.
